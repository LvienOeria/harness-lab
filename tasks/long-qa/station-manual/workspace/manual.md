# Deep Space Station 7 Operations Manual

## Section 1. Emergency power
In a main-reactor failure, auxiliary batteries provide power for exactly 90 minutes.
If the emergency beacon is enabled, battery life drops to 45 minutes.
Section 1.1 says: never open the reactor hatch during a blue-alert drill.

## Section 2. Life support
Oxygen recycling is rated for 12 crew members. With more than 15 crew members,
carbon-dioxide scrubbers must be switched to high mode. High mode consumes 3.2 kW.

## Section 3. Docking
A standard cargo shuttle may dock only at airlocks A, B, or C. Airlock B is reserved
for medical evac between 06:00 and 18:00 station time.

## Section 4. Communication
The long-range antenna uses the 7.4 GHz band. A storm warning is broadcast every
30 minutes on channel 9 when solar wind speed exceeds 600 km/s.
