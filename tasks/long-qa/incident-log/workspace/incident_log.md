# Incident Log: Station Gamma

## Incident 12
At 03:12 station time, the cooling loop lost pressure. Engineer Mara shut valve A and routed coolant through the secondary loop. The repair took 41 minutes.

## Incident 15
At 22:40, a micrometeoroid breached panel 7. The emergency seal deployed automatically. Airlock C was locked down for two hours.

## Incident 19
At 06:05, the greenhouse lights failed. Botanist Ivo replaced relay R-9 with a spare from locker 4. The crops were without light for 18 minutes.

## Incident 22
At 11:58, a fire alarm sounded in corridor B. The fire was caused by a shorted battery. Crew used foam units 2 and 5. Corridor B reopened at 13:20.
